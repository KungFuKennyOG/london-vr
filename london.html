The Bag

Degsy's got a bag on the boot of his motor. It needs to get across Bethnal Green, and the Old Bill are walking their beats. Don't run. Don't let them see it. After that the manor's yours: Ali's deliveries, the Roof Run, Degsy's stashes on the rooftops.

Phone: thumbstick to walk, drag to look, RUN, GRAB and JUMP (landscape works best). Desktop: mouse to look, WASD to walk, Shift to run, E or click to grab, Space to vault or climb, hold T to wind the clock. Headset: left stick to walk (click it to run), right stick to turn, squeeze with a hand on the bag to grab it, A or X to vault or climb. Look at someone up close to meet them.

# London VR: handoff

Read this and `QUALITY.md` at the start of every chat in this Project. To start a new chat: "continue London VR from KungFuKennyOG/stairwell, branch `london-art-set`".

## What it is
- **Working title:** London VR (no name yet).
- **The game:** an on-foot VR crime caper for Meta Quest 3, played in the browser through WebXR.
  - You are Danny "Two Sugars" Doyle, 19, a wannabe gangster from the Hollis House estate in Bethnal Green.
  - The tone is Guy Ritchie: rival crews, jobs that go wrong, freeze-frame character intros, a cocky voiceover.
- **The loop:** take a job, it goes wrong, heat rises, you escape on foot, you lie low, you get paid.
- **The world:** a compressed East London of dense hubs.
  - **The estate** (Hackney / Bethnal Green) is the start.
  - **The City** is reached on foot through Shoreditch's back streets.
  - **The docks** (Limehouse) are reached on foot along the Regent's Canal.
  - **Soho** is reached only by Tube or cab.
- **The Concept doc** in this Project has the full pitch, the crews, the wanted levels and the milestones.

## Decisions made (and why)
- **Parkour is free movement, never a context prompt** (Kenny, Nov 5). The on-screen hint only says what a jump would do; it never gates it.
- **On foot, no driving.** Driving is the biggest motion-sickness risk and the biggest chunk of work, and a dense city reads better on foot.
- **Light parkour only:** climbing, vaulting, short gap jumps and ziplines. These are hand-driven, short moves that stay comfortable in VR. No wall-running and no sliding.
- **Dense hubs, not one big map,** with compressed geography: real names and fake distances, 2–3 minute walks between neighbouring areas, and Tube or cab rides (as a passenger) for the rest. This keeps scope and performance manageable, and each hub loads as its own level.
- **It should feel big, not be big.** Use skyline impostors, verticality, enterable interiors, a mostly-closed Tube map and street sound. Each hub should take 3–5 minutes to cross.
- **Interiors come in three tiers:**
  - Fully enterable, when a space serves a job, an escape or a hideout.
  - Traversal routes (stairwells, walkways).
  - Fake window interiors for everything else.
- **Engine: the Stairwell engine** (Kenny, Nov 5, confirmed twice). It brings his skinned, painted character rig plus melee, grabs, weapons and a working Quest WebXR path. A full three.js rebuild was tried the same day and shelved (see Status).
- **Look: Stairwell's PS1 style as a house style, London's own world** (Kenny, Nov 5). This reverses the earlier "not Stairwell" ruling. Without the PS1 pass the engine looked bare, the bleach-bypass film look didn't save it, and semi-real procedural people looked creepy. Identity now comes from the palette, signage, dress, street furniture and the freeze-frame.
- **Characters: Stairwell's rig with London clothes** (Kenny, Nov 5). The London kit v1 (sculpted heads, five-finger hands) is removed.
- **The freeze-frame is a character intro, not a world stop** (Kenny, Nov 5: the grey-out "doesn't make much sense"). Only the character being introduced holds still while their card shows, and the colours stay normal. It triggers on look (within 4 m, 0.6 s of gaze), once per character.
- **The shared engine is never edited for London.** Look overrides live in `src/london/40-render.js` (it replaces `MAIN` and draws through its own post pass), so Stairwell's pages are unaffected. Exception (Nov 5, signs): `glTex` gained an opt-in `t.smooth` flag (mipmaps and anisotropy) and a `retex()` helper. Nothing changes unless a texture sets the flag, so Stairwell's pages render exactly as before.
- **The calibration anchor waits for the first full iteration of the level** (Kenny). Until then, scores are provisional against BAYHEM Level 3 (6.2).

## Structure (branch `london-art-set`)
- `src/london/00-head.html`: page shell, CSS and HUD in London VR's own kit (Anton + Courier Prime). Look switches: Night grade, PS1, Rain.
- `src/london/40-render.js`: London's renderer.
  - `MAIN` gets screen-door transparency: a material with `cut = 2` is dithered by its alpha.
  - `drawLondon` renders at `state.sharp` × the canvas (the XR loop does the same per eye) with a linear upscale, then `POST_LDN` applies the London palette and the freeze-frame (`LOOK.freeze`), then the 15-bit dither.
- `src/london/45-cast.js`: HEADS (painted face specs), painted garments, props, `ROSTER` and `ORDER` (all on Stairwell's rig via `makeChar`).
- `src/london/46-street.js`: the street.
  - Texture painters and builder helpers: `slab`, `wallP` (`whole: true` = no tiling), `boxW` (world-scale UVs), `shopFront(x, w, kind)`.
  - Rain volume (`rainTick`) and `STREET_LIGHT` (8 point lights maximum; slot 6 is the freeze-frame fill).
- `src/london/47-dressing.js`: the dressing and the east vista (see Status). `DRESS.tick(t, eye)` runs from the loop; `streak(x, z, y, colour, len, width)` adds a wet-road reflection.
- `src/london/49-ui.js`: camera (orbit plus eye-level walk), cast placement (`SPOTS`), freeze-frames, card and loop. `window.__london` exposes hooks for capture, including `setClock(t)`.
- `build.sh`: the `london.html` block. It concatenates `src/10-engine.js`, `src/houses/40-helpers.js`, `src/houses/43-castkit.js` and `src/london/*.js`.
- `tools/london_shots.py <outdir>`: the six fixed review frames (see `QUALITY.md`). `tools/london_cam.py <out.jpg> '<setup js>' ...`: any other view.
- `review/london/passN/`: each pass's frames, plus `stairwell_ref.jpg` for the identity check.

## Open issues, by impact (from the pass 2 review, 5.0)
1. **The world is a thin, dark strip.** The deck block over the shops reads as a black band, and there are only two lit shopfronts.
   - Swap the brutalist deck for three-storey brick terraces with seeded sash windows.
   - Add three more distinct fronts (bookies, launderette, kebab shop) and seed the shutter tags so no two match.
   - Push more sodium into the palette.
   - *Done in pass 3:* more lit windows, and a sodium-orange sky.
2. **Text that doesn't fit.**
   - *Done in pass 3:* menu boards and the freeze card.
   - Still to do: the small sub-lines under each fascia are mush at 480 lines. Make them at least 14 px tall at that resolution, or drop them.
3. **Freeze staging.**
   - *Done in pass 3:* the walk-up stops at 1.5 m, and the card sits at the shoulder on a black plate with the verdict wrapping.
   - Check it in the headset, where the field of view is much wider.
4. **Anatomy at 0.6 m.**
   - The head is cylinder-straight, the nose is a hard-lit wedge, the ear is a flat card, and the neck is too long and too wide. The cap sits high, shoulders are cubes, and hands are mittens.
   - Fix on Stairwell's rig: taper the head, shorten the neck about 20%, use lathe shoulders for the puffer, and pose fists round the props.
5. **The chicken shop.**
   - *Done in pass 3:* tiled back wall, readable menus, rooster logo, and Ali behind the counter.
   - Still to do: glass with screen-door reflections, and a heated display with steam.
6. **Steam** reads as a dithered column; keep it small and soft or drop it.
7. **Headset checks pending:** fps at the heaviest view, scale at 1:1, and comfort. `london.html` has no WebXR entry yet.
